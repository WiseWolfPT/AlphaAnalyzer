WHY BLOCK DCF FOR BANKS?
-------------------------
Banks have negative/erratic Free Cash Flow due to:
  1. Regulatory capital requirements (Basel III)
  2. Business model = cash flow mechanism (deposits/lending)
  3. Alternative financing (deposits, interbank)
  4. FCF meaningless for valuation
