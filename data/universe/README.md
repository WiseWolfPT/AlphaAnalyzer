Place CSV files here with one record per line:

symbol,company_name,exchange,sector,industry

Examples (header optional):

SP500.csv
AAPL,Apple Inc.,NASDAQ,Technology,Consumer Electronics
MSFT,Microsoft Corp.,NASDAQ,Technology,Software
...

Other files you can add:
- NASDAQ100.csv
- DOW30.csv
- PSI20.csv
- IBEX35.csv
- DAX.csv
- CAC40.csv
- FTSE100.csv
- EUROPE_TOP200.csv
- ADR_ASIA_TOP100.csv
- RUSSELL2000_TOP200.csv
- CRYPTO_TECH_GROWTH.csv

Comments starting with # are ignored. Duplicate symbols are deduplicated at the DB level.
